Transire - Your One-Stop Hotel Booking App
Welcome to Transire, your go-to hotel booking app designed to simplify and enhance your travel experience. StayEase offers a seamless platform for users to discover, compare, and book accommodations with ease, ensuring a comfortable stay wherever your journey takes you.

Features
Web View Integration: Seamlessly browse and book accommodations within the app.
Login and Registration System: Secure access to your account for personalized booking experiences.
Booking System: Easily book your preferred hotels with just a few taps.
Google Map Integration: View hotel locations and nearby amenities for informed decisions.
Hotel Selection Category: Filter and explore hotels based on your preferences.
Gallery Browsing: Visualize hotel facilities and rooms before making a booking.
Technologies Used
Android Studio: Development environment for creating the Android app.
Firebase: Backend services for user authentication and data storage.
Java: Programming language for Android app development.
